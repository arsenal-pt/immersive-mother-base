This is an installable **testing alpha**, not a claim that every planned activity or interior works. Missing native service/group/CQC resource actions remain disabled. The experimental platform has known collision/material failures and is not installed with the core; archived crash variants are not recommended for gameplay. Read the version-specific status before rebuilding.

The GitHub-generated "Source code" archives are source snapshots. The named **core.zip** is the prepared installer download. No extracted game packs/models/textures/Blender files, framework DLLs, saves or personal paths are supplied. Input hashes verify compatible stock resources; they do not independently prove purchase/ownership. Use a legally obtained game.
